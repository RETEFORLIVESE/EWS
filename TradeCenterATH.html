Visitor Information

The Trade Center is at the southern end of Manhattan. Subway and PATH trains stop at the Concourse level, under the Plaza.

Getting here

  1. Take the subway or PATH train to the Trade Center station.
  2. Follow the signs to the Concourse.
  3. Take the escalators up to the Plaza.
Tip: the Concourse is indoors, so it is a good place to wait out the rain.
